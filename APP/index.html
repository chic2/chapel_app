A Legacy of
Devotion

Since Foundation

Founded as the spiritual heart of Augustine University, the Chapel of Mary, Mother of the Church, serves as a sanctuary where students and faculty alike find solace. From its modest beginnings to becoming a beacon of architectural and spiritual beauty, the chapel has always prioritized the holistic development of the human person.

Our mission is simple: To provide a welcoming space for prayer, sacramental life, and intellectual inquiry, ensuring that every member of the AUI community feels the presence of Pure Grace.

Our Mission

The Chapel of Mary, Mother of the Church, stands as the spiritual lighthouse of Augustine University. Our mission is to foster a community where Faith and Intellect converge.

Through this digital portal, we bring daily liturgy, sacramental growth, and departmental fellowship to every student's fingertips, ensuring the "AUI Spirit" thrives beyond the physical walls.
